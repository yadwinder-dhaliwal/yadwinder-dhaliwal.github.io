Project Selene In progress

Oct 2026 – now · solo
  • C++20
  • OpenGL
  • GLSL
  • Dear ImGui
  • Python/GDAL

Lunar site modeler for the south-pole terrain around Shackleton crater, built from NASA LOLA elevation data. I implemented B-spline curves and bicubic surfaces from scratch and fit them with a separable least-squares solver using a hand-written Cholesky decomposition, backed by unit tests against finite differences and exact bicubic reproduction.

On a 601×601 grid, RMS error drops from 159 m to 7.7 m (8×8 → 66×66 control points) with a 3.2 ms fit; the 1.18M-triangle surface renders at 240 FPS.

RaySense In progress

Oct 2026 – now · solo
  • C++20
  • Vulkan ray queries
  • OpenUSD
  • PhysX 5
  • ROS 2 Jazzy

GPU LiDAR and camera sensor simulator for robotics testing. It ray-traces sensor returns with Vulkan ray queries over OpenUSD scenes and publishes ROS 2 PointCloud2 and Image messages, so robot software can consume simulated data as if it came from real hardware.

Target: a 64-beam LiDAR at 10 Hz in real time on an RTX 5070. Vulkan, CMake and Ubuntu toolchain are in place.

FormulaInfinite

Jan – Apr 2026 · team of 5
  • C++20
  • PhysX 5 Vehicle2
  • OpenGL/GLSL
  • CMake

Real-time racing engine with procedurally generated tracks. I owned vehicle physics: cooking road meshes into welded triangle-mesh colliders, velocity-squared downforce and drag every frame, and per-wheel tire friction behind a bounds-checked API after root-causing a bug where all four wheels shared one table. I also built the speed, RPM and gear telemetry HUD.

Delivered the first build where the car drove on a generated track; grip rises up to 4.2% at 1,470 N downforce with a fixed 1.9 : 1.6 front-to-rear ratio.

Anti Smurf (FairQueue) In progress

Oct 2026 – now · team of 2

With Guntaas Uppal

  • Python
  • Simulation
  • Elo / TrueSkill
  • Anomaly detection
  • pytest

Smurf detection for matchmaking. Real match data almost never says who is a smurf, so detectors can't be checked. We simulate first: every player has a hidden true skill and a visible rating, smurfs are injected with known labels, and rating models and detectors are scored against that ground truth.

Done: Elo baseline and a labelled player simulator. Next: TrueSkill, skill-trajectory features, anomaly detection and a queue simulator that weighs wait time against match fairness.
